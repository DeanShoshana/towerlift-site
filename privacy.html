This policy describes how TowerLift Sky Builder ("the app"), published by Dean Shoshana, handles information. In short: there are no user accounts, we hold no personal data on our own servers, and we do not sell your personal information for money. To keep the app free, it shows ads through Google AdMob, which — with your permission — may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize those ads and measure them across apps. You can decline this at any time, and California residents can opt out of this "sharing" (see the California section below).

Information we do not collect

The app has no user accounts and no registration. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any content you create. Your scores, coins, hearts, settings and progress are stored locally on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve ads and measure their performance, Google may collect and process data from your device, including:

  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from it
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic data used for fraud prevention and frequency capping

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can decline.

If you purchase Remove Ads, the app stops showing forced ads (such as the interstitial after a failed run). Optional rewarded ads that you deliberately choose to watch remain available and strictly opt-in.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and the advertising identifier

On iOS, the app presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure their performance, including across other companies' apps and websites — activity that Apple defines as "tracking." If you decline, or have not responded to the prompt, the app requests non-personalized ads and does not use the advertising identifier to track you. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Because tracking is possible for users who opt in, the app's App Store privacy label discloses data that may be "used to track you."

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details — Apple handles billing under its own privacy policy. Purchase entitlements (such as Remove Ads) are verified through Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best scores are submitted to the TowerLift leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can disable Game Center in iOS Settings.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA. We do not sell your personal information for money. However, when you opt in to personalized ads, the app shares online identifiers (such as your advertising identifier) with Google to show ads relevant to you. California law may treat this as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, or as a "sale."

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. You can opt out of this sharing at any time by declining Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, or by turning tracking off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Once you opt out, the app requests only non-personalized ads and the advertising identifier is not used to track you. You may also email shoshanadean@gmail.com to exercise your California rights — including the right to know, delete, or correct personal information — and you will not be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers we know to be under 16 years of age.

Children

The app is a casual game suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where age signals are available, ad requests are configured accordingly.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data on our own servers. Data processed by Google or Apple is governed by their policies, where you can also exercise your rights of access and deletion. Deleting the app removes all locally stored progress.
