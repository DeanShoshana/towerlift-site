Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy describes how TowerLift Sky Builder, published by Dean Shoshana, handles information. TowerLift uses Google AdMob for advertising and Apple services for purchases and Game Center. Google may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize and measure ads across apps and websites when you grant tracking permission and your consent choices allow it.

Information stored on your device

TowerLift works without a TowerLift account. The app stores your scores, coins, hearts, settings and progress on your device. If you use Game Center, Apple also receives the game results described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve ads and measure their performance, Google may collect and process data from your device, including:

  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from it
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic data used for fraud prevention and frequency capping

Google explains its handling of this data in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data practices. Depending on your location, Google’s consent platform may present additional advertising privacy choices.

The Remove Ads purchase removes ads between runs and includes one free revive per run. You can choose to watch rewarded ads for other bonuses.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and the advertising identifier

TowerLift uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to request permission to track. When you allow tracking and your consent choices allow it, Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for ad personalization and measurement across other apps and websites. If tracking permission is denied or unavailable, Google Mobile Ads omits the IDFA from ad requests. You may still see ads. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details — Apple handles billing under its own privacy policy. Purchase entitlements (such as Remove Ads) are verified through Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple’s Game Center, TowerLift can send Apple your leaderboard results and achievement progress. Apple operates Game Center under Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage or turn off Game Center in iOS Settings.

Advertising privacy choices

Use the consent choices presented by Google where available and manage TowerLift’s tracking permission in iOS Settings. To make a privacy request, including a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, email shoshanadean@gmail.com.

Children

The app is a casual game suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where age signals are available, ad requests are configured accordingly.

Data retention and your rights

TowerLift does not run a server for player accounts or progression. Deleting the app removes the progress stored in its app data on that device. Apple and Google describe their retention practices and privacy request options in their own policies. You can also use the contact below to make a request about TowerLift.

Changes
